# About Wanjaaro

**Wanjaaro** is an independent technology platform dedicated to building fast, focused, and privacy-conscious web applications and digital tools.

---

## Our Mission

Our mission is straightforward: **build high-performance web utilities that are fast, accessible, and respect user privacy.**

Everything runs directly in your browser. We do not require accounts, we do not lock features behind paywalls, and your input data never leaves your device.

---

## Core Principles

- **Client-Side Speed** — Fast and responsive experiences that run in your browser.
- **Privacy by Design** — Zero personal data collection or tracking.
- **Simplicity & Accessibility** — Clean interfaces designed for clarity and ease of use.
- **No Friction** — Free access with no forced sign-ups or downloads.
