# About Wanjaaros – Free Financial Calculators for Real Life

**Wanjaaros** is a growing collection of free, browser-based financial calculators built for anyone who wants to make smarter money decisions—whether you're saving for your first home, planning for retirement, or tackling debt. Our tools help you run the numbers clearly and confidently, no matter where you are in your financial journey.

---

## Our Mission

Good financial decisions start with clear numbers. But too often, people rely on guesswork, outdated rules, or confusing spreadsheets. We're here to change that.

Our mission is simple: **make accurate financial calculations fast, easy, and accessible to everyone.**

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your income, balances, and personal details never leave your device. We don't store, process, or transmit your financial data—because your privacy matters just as much as your financial planning.

---

## What We Offer

We provide **170+ unique financial calculators**, organized into eight practical categories:

- **Growth** — Compound interest, simple interest, savings goals, high-yield savings, Rule of 72, dividend reinvestment, dollar-cost averaging, and investment tools.
- **Retirement** — 401(k), IRA, Roth IRA, withdrawal rates, and FIRE planning.
- **Mortgage** — Payments, rent vs. buy, refinancing, affordability, payoff planning, closing costs, down payments, and mortgage points.
- **Debt** — Loan payoff, credit card payoff, debt snowball, debt avalanche, amortization, student loan payoff, debt consolidation, and balance transfers.
- **Insurance** — Life insurance, disability, term vs. whole life, deductible break-even, home/renters, and umbrella coverage.
- **Tax** — Salary, hourly pay, overtime, take-home pay, tax brackets, capital gains, and self-employment tax.
- **Budgeting** — 50/30/20 budget, net worth, debt-to-income, emergency fund, subscription tracking, sinking funds, and zero-based budgeting.
- **Investing** — Position sizing for stocks, crypto, options, and futures, plus risk/reward ratio and Kelly Criterion tools.

Every tool is **free to use**, requires **no registration**, and works on **any modern browser**—desktop, tablet, or mobile.

## Our Commitment to You

- **Accuracy** — All calculators use industry-standard formulas you can trust.
- **Privacy** — Zero data collection. Nothing is stored or shared.

## Who's Behind Wanjaaros?

I'm **Sohail Anwar**, a software engineer with over a decade of experience—since 2013—building enterprise-grade applications for the finance, banking, and insurance industries. My work has covered mortgage underwriting, loan repurchase tracking, insurance claims processing, and regulatory compliance systems, built with Java, Spring Boot, and modern web frameworks for institutions that rely on these systems handling real transactions at scale.

That background is exactly why Wanjaaros exists.

Years spent working directly with the logic behind mortgages, amortization, claims, and financial workflows convinced me that these calculations should be **transparent, accurate, and available to anyone**—without subscriptions or paywalls getting in the way.

**A quick note:** Wanjaaros isn't a financial advisory service, and nothing here is personalized financial advice. It's a set of precise, formula-based tools built by someone who has spent years engineering these exact systems professionally. If you're making a major financial decision, always confirm details with your bank, lender, insurer, or a licensed financial advisor. Wanjaaros is here to help you run the numbers quickly and clearly along the way.
